What is Doc2FAQ?

Doc2FAQ is a web application that takes your uploaded documents and automatically generates a set of frequently asked questions (FAQs) from the content. Whether you have user manuals, policy documents, or knowledge-base articles, Doc2FAQ helps you surface the most important information quickly and clearly.

Planned Features

  • 📄
    Document Ingestion

    Upload PDFs, Word documents, and plain-text files for processing.

  • 🤖
    Automatic FAQ Generation

    Intelligent extraction of questions and answers from your content.

  • 🌐
    Web-Based Interface

    Manage and browse your generated FAQs from any browser.
